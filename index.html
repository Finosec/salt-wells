Salt Wells

The Darkening · A Book One Wayside

Week two on the road east. The condenser cracked at dawn and you are three days dry. A waymark totem points down a dead off-ramp to a highway town that should have water — and is far too quiet. Sundown is coming. So is something else.

Your hands, and theirs

You play Sally Adler, seventeen, the Compass cold in your jacket. Lina and Rami are at your shoulders. Three gifts between you — and every one of them bites the hand that uses it.

▸ Read the Threads — your Strategic Intuition. Glimpse where a choice leads before you take it. Costs Strain. Lean on it too hard and the world stops feeling real.

▸ Push — Rami's Kinetic Adaptation. Speed and force past human limit. Costs Strain. His nerves are already fraying.

▸ Reach — Lina's Empathic Resonance. Feel the living and the lost; quiet a Broken; read a stranger's intent. Costs Strain. What she feels, she keeps.

Strain is shared. Fill the bar and you don't just lose — you slide. There are many ways out of Salt Wells. Most of them are bad. Find a good one. Then find a better one.
